Ruling (lernie bl-454d, 2026-10-22): a procedure child is not a conversation and never appears in a seat's conversation list. Today nothing on disk distinguishes a titler, a compactor or a verifier from a subagent the model dispatched with the dispatch tool: both write 'dispatch: <role> [<id>]'. The fact is which binding forked it, and run_procedure is its one door, so the dispatch commit body carries one trailer, 'Procedure: <event-key>'. The subject grammar does not move (role::derive, founding_sha unchanged); legacy commits lack the trailer and read as not-a-procedure, which is right for everything a model dispatched and tolerable for old compactors until retention. yog reads the trailer off the founding commit (yog bl-009a). Machinery (DESIGN_PROCEDURES §2) is a narrower set; this records the wider fact the seat needs.
