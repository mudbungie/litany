Round-1 daily lane. litany 0.0.10 under yog 0.0.38 (main c7916da1), seat lernie 0.1.30, worker `claude-sonnet-5` via `claude-session-direct`.

A bare stop reports success, the conversation goes `stopped` for one poll, and then it is running again and spending.

Measured, on a root conversation that had dispatched compactor children. Each line is one `lernie agent` poll five seconds apart, printing state, cumulative tokens, and each seat's `doing`:

    --- before ---
    in-flight 1568097 ['thinking','idle','idle','idle','idle','inference']
    --- lernie stop <ws> <agent>  ->  {"exit":0,"kind":"outcome","ok":true,"stderr":"","stdout":""}
    stopped   1741354 ['idle','idle','idle','idle','idle','inference']
    in-flight 1817892 ['thinking','idle','idle','idle','idle','idle']
    in-flight 1951459 ['waiting','idle','idle','idle','idle','idle']
    live      1997017 ['idle','idle','idle','idle','idle','idle']
    in-flight 2134232 ['waiting','idle','idle','idle','idle','idle']
    in-flight 2180111 ['thinking','idle','idle','idle','idle','idle']
    in-flight 2272169 ['waiting','idle','idle','idle','idle','idle']
    in-flight 2364619 ['thinking','idle','idle','idle','idle','idle']

796,522 tokens spent after the stop said ok. The op yog spawned is the right one — the trail row is

    litany stop <workspace> <agent>   [exit 0]

so nothing is lost in translation; the bare form simply does not hold.

Only the cascade holds. Firing the flag the boundary carries and the seat spells no word for:

    lernie ask '{"op":"stop","workspace":"...","agent":"...","children":true}'
    -> stopped, and it stays stopped

Reproduced twice on two different conversations in two workspaces, both with live children. Total burned across the lane while establishing it: about 7M tokens.

Two readings, and it matters which:

1. The children are what re-wake the root — the shipped `workflow.yaml` binds `compactor_return: land_compaction`, so a compactor finishing after the root's driver died lands and the branch advances again. If that is it, then a bare stop on a conversation with children is never the gesture anyone wants, and the engine knows it: yog's own records reply already says "the engine offers nudge, stop with its children".
2. Or the kill races the executor's own re-drive. A driver SIGKILLed by hand behaved the same way in this lane — a new `yog litany advance` was up within a minute with no ops row — which is good resilience, and is the same mechanism seen from the other side.

Expected: `stop` stops, or it refuses and says a cascade is required. What it may not do is report `ok` and leave the conversation spending, because the operator's next act after a successful stop is to walk away.

Severity p1: it is the one control an operator reaches for when something is wrong, it is the control every runaway-cost story ends at, and it silently does nothing on exactly the conversations that have gone wrong (the ones with children).
