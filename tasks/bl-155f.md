Round-1 swdev lane, litany 0.0.10 under yog 0.0.38, worker `claude-sonnet-5` via `claude-session-direct`.

Scenario: an interactive conversation on a small Rust crate. After the first reply I deposited a change of requirement — 'from_roman must return Result<u32, ParseRomanError> instead of Option, with a public error type that names the offending position. Update the tests. Then also add a Display impl for the error.' The driver woke, ran to step 012, and the conversation ended having written nothing.

What `lernie steps` shows:

    009 complete     none 27696 274
    010 complete     none 28370 122
    011 complete     none 29412 644
    012 output_limit none 30874 4100

and the step's own `request.json` opens `"max_tokens": 4096`.

Two facts behind it:

1. **The cap is a hardcoded constant with no config surface.** `src/prompt/dispatch/step_commit.rs:54`: `pub(super) const DEFAULT_MAX_TOKENS: u32 = 4096;`. Its own doc comment distinguishes it from the §6 spend budgets and the §5.2 manifest `budget_tokens`, which is right, but nothing in `manifest.yaml`, `providers.yaml` or a workflow can move it. The provider row cannot either: this workspace's wall carries `body_defaults = { max_tokens = 32000 }` on its `claude-session-direct` row, and the request still went out at 4096, because the canonical request sets the field explicitly and `body_defaults` only fills what is absent. So an operator who has read brazen's config surface and set the value they want gets no effect and no notice.

2. **The outcome is not surfaced as a failure.** The conversation's state is `stopped`. `lernie attention` renders it beside six healthy ones as:

       ShorelineGuppy    stopped ['stopped'] | came to rest — your turn | failure: None

   `failure: None`, `signals: ['stopped']`, and a sentence — 'came to rest — your turn' — that is the exact sentence a conversation that finished its work gets. The only place the truncation is named at all is the `framing` column of `lernie steps`, which is a drill-in nobody opens for a conversation that says it came to rest.

Expected: a step cut off by the output cap is a wound, or at least a distinct signal and sentence — 'cut off at the output cap' — because the operator's next act differs completely (nudge to continue, or raise the cap) from the act after a real rest. And the cap wants a config home, since 4096 is small for a role whose job is writing files: this one died writing a Rust error type and its tests.

Severity p2: silent, repeatable, and it wastes the whole step's output (4100 tokens generated, nothing committed). It is p1-shaped for anyone who cannot see `lernie steps`, because from the seat the conversation simply stopped doing what it was told.
