Found while closing bl-bc51 (round-1 usability fix lane L3). `make check` fails on `main` for every worktree in the repo:

    CHANGELOG.md has no bullet for 1 deliveries in litany-v0.0.9..refs/heads/main: bl-0644

`tests/changelog_completeness.rs`'s `delivery_id` exempts a release-prep commit with `subject.contains("release prep:")`. But a delivery subject is the ball's TITLE, squashed by `bl close` — nobody types it to a convention. bl-0644's title is *"promote the accumulated [Unreleased] section to 0.0.10, the version the open release PR proposes"*, which is the same act under different words, so the exemption missed and the guard now demands a bullet the changelog header explicitly says must not be written ("a bullet saying the changelog was promoted is noise in the very release notes it is promoting").

It blocks every close in the repo until it is fixed, and it cannot be fixed by writing the bullet: writing it puts the noise in 0.0.11's notes and teaches the next promotion to do the same.

EXPECTED: the exemption is derived from what the commit DID, not from how its subject was worded. The promotion landing is recognizable without a phrase: its diff touches `CHANGELOG.md` and nothing else, and it ADDS a `## [x.y.z](...)` version heading. A gate close stays a subject convention, because that is what a gate close is.
