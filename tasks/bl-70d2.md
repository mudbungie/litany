Follow-up to bl-b902 (ARCH §5.5's classification names this the one prefix edit that is neither chosen for its cost nor a subtraction). Today load_skill copies skills/<name>/ into the worktree and the next assembly composes it in the body's skills/** category (§5.2), ahead of the whole transcript — so the whole transcript is re-sent on every later step until a compaction landing rebuilds. Design: compose a loaded body as a path-framed block in the TAIL, adjoined to the tool_result of the load_skill call that loaded it (the anchor is derivable from the assistant entry's tool_use block: name load_skill, input.name), and exclude tail-anchored skills from the body's skills/** walk; at a landing the span deletion removes the anchor with the entries before the compaction point, and the skill composes in the body from the tree — the transcript is the queue and the landing is the fold, no new state. A load is then an append, byte-monotone like every other tool result. Constraints: src/prompt/dispatch/assembler/body.rs is at 290 lines and must be split along its select/fit seam before it gains the exclusion; the anchored block composes from the current tree (an rm of a tail-anchored skill flushes from the anchor, not from the body — cheaper than today, priced in §5.4); a second load of an already-loaded skill anchors nothing (already_loaded). Extend src/prompt/tests/prefix_ladder.rs with a load step and assert the prefix stays whole across it. Amend ARCH §3.3 Body-on-demand, §5.2, §5.5 (retire the 'inserts into the body and pays the flush from its position' clause) and docs/DESIGN_CONTEXT_ECONOMY.md §11.
